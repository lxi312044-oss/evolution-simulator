# Phase 06.2.12: Energy-aware hunting policy

Goal: determine whether premature satiety stops carnivores from obtaining enough food even when herbivores remain abundant. No population rescue or artificial energy creation.

## Conditions (same seeds, 10 × 50,000 ticks each)

- `control`: unchanged 06.2.11 unified feeding.
- `early-50`: resume hunting at stomach fullness <= 50% rather than 25%.
- `reserve-aware`: if reserve energy < 3 E, allow hunting despite satiety, **only if both stomach E and N have free capacity**.
- `forecast-aware`: if reserve E + stomach E < 300 ticks of predicted basal + full-speed movement cost, allow hunting despite satiety, only with stomach capacity.

The stop-at-80% hysteresis remains unchanged. The override does not bypass attack cooldown, stomach capacity, or prey refuge. Digestive and resource-transfer paths are unchanged. `hunting_hunger_override_ticks` counts candidate overrides, `hunting_hunger_override_no_capacity_ticks` counts those blocked by capacity, and `hunting_hunger_override_attacks` counts actual attacks during overrides. All counters are cumulative.
