# Phase 06.2.16 — Carnivore reproduction bottleneck diagnostics

## Purpose
Find why carnivores reproduce too little. **No forced survival or replenishment.**

## Controlled experiment
All settings and seed values are identical except carnivore `offspring_energy`:

| mode | offspring_energy | reproduction_work_energy |
|---|---:|---:|
| control | 22.0 | 2.0 |
| energy-80 | 17.6 | 2.0 |
| energy-60 | 13.2 | 2.0 |

The `offspring_energy` is transferred from parent to offspring (not generated). The 2.0 work energy remains a cost. Changing offspring energy can alter child survival and long-term ecosystem dynamics; results are hypotheses to test, not expected improvements.

## Diagnostics
Existing mutually prioritized physical failure counters: cooldown, age, body nutrients, reserve nutrients, energy, world capacity. Other independent gates: food probability, prey trend, stomach fullness, prey/predator sustainability. New cumulative counters: `repro_disabled_ticks`, `repro_physical_ready_ticks`, `repro_all_gates_passed_ticks`. `repro_ready` is retained for compatibility and should equal physical ready when reproduction is enabled. Each count is an individual-tick, **not a number of distinct animals**. `mean_carnivore_age` and `mean_carnivore_stomach_energy` are sampled averages for living carnivores; zero when none live. `carnivore_age_deaths` and `carnivore_energy_deaths` are cumulative counts.
