# Phase 06.2.21: Herbivore energy utilization diagnostics

This phase adds **observational counters only**. Ecology parameters and reproduction/feeding behavior remain unchanged.

## Energy flow

Plant reserve E -> herbivore reserve E (feeding); herbivore reserve E -> heat (basal and movement); parent reserve E -> offspring reserve E (reproduction transfer); parent reserve E -> heat (reproduction work). Stomach holds **nutrient N, not energy E** in the herbivore model. Digestion transfers N from stomach to reserve; feeding energy is credited directly to reserve.

## New cumulative CSV columns

* `phase21_live_samples`, `phase21_live_reserve_e_total`, `phase21_live_reserve_n_total`, `phase21_live_stomach_n_total`: per-animal-tick pre-metabolism snapshots. Divide totals by live_samples to obtain sample-weighted means; these are not unique-individual means.
* `phase21_live_zero_e_samples`, `phase21_live_energy_gate_ready_samples`: snapshot counts.
* `phase21_basal_e_requested`, `phase21_basal_e_unpaid`: metabolic demand and unmet demand when reserves are empty.
* `phase21_movement_e_requested`, `phase21_movement_e_unpaid`: hypothetical requested movement cost and unfunded distance cost; actual paid movement remains `phase19_herbivore_movement_e`.
* `phase21_starvation_hp_damage`, `phase21_starvation_damage_ticks`: starvation damage events (not a definitive cause-of-death label).
* `phase21_feeding_plant_energy_available`, `phase21_feeding_energy_capacity_blocked`: plant E available for feeding and amount blocked by reserve capacity (counterfactual, not transferred).
* `phase21_reproduction_offspring_e_transferred`, `phase21_reproduction_work_e`: offspring E transfer and work E dissipation.
* `phase21_death_*`: reserves at removal, with zero/nonzero energy counts; death may have multiple causes.
* `phase21_repro_only_energy_blocked`, `phase21_repro_multiple_gates_blocked`: energy gate deficit with/without additional observable gate failures, excluding stochastic food gate and population cap.

All phase21 counters are observation-only; they do not debit or credit any resource. Beware that `phase21_live_*` snapshots are before basal metabolism but after digestion, and `phase21_feeding_energy_capacity_blocked` is an opportunity metric, not conservation-ledger energy.
