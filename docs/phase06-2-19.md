# Phase 06.2.19 — food chain supply diagnostics

Baseline: phase06.2.18 basal-60 (predator basal energy 0.018). Five conditions, five seeds, 50,000 ticks each.

- control: unchanged baseline
- plant-120: seed_probability 0.65 -> 0.78
- plant-150: seed_probability 0.65 -> 0.975
- herbivore-120: herbivore reproduction_cooldown_ticks 600 -> 500
- combined: plant-120 and herbivore-120

New CSV columns: plant births/age deaths/consumed deaths, herbivore births/age deaths/HP deaths, herbivore HP-death split by energy reserve (zero vs positive), plant N consumption, herbivore feeding E/basal E/movement E, plant uptake N and photosynthesis E. All counters are cumulative; use differences between rows for interval events.

**Caveat**: HP death with zero reserve energy is a proxy, not confirmed starvation. HP death with positive energy is not necessarily predation. True cause-of-death instrumentation would require damage-source tracking. The herbivore reproduction cooldown is shortened by 16.7% (600/1.2), not a direct 20% increase in actual births. No forced respawn, no injected N/E. Existing ledger conservation is preserved.

Run: `cargo test --workspace`, then `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_phase06219.ps1 -Ticks 1000 -SeedCount 1` and full `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_phase06219.ps1`. Outputs: `results/phase06219/`.
