# Phase 06.2.22 — Herbivore reproduction-gate intervention study

## Objective
Identify which reproduction requirements prevent herbivore recovery after plant rebound, without adding organisms or violating N/E conservation. This phase changes **only configuration values**; Rust simulation mechanics and CSV diagnostics are inherited from 06.2.21.

## Conditions (all share the 06.2.21 control baseline)

| Mode | Energy threshold fraction | Offspring body N | Satiety threshold | Well-fed ticks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| control | 0.80 | 12.0 | 0.50 | 100 |
| energy-80 | 0.64 | 12.0 | 0.50 | 100 |
| body-n-80 | 0.80 | 9.6 | 0.50 | 100 |
| satiety-80 | 0.80 | 12.0 | 0.40 | 80 |
| combined | 0.64 | 9.6 | 0.40 | 80 |

The term `body-n-80` reduces the *offspring body N transfer requirement*, not a separate body-N gate. `satiety-80` changes two related satiety parameters and cannot distinguish their individual effects. All conditions use the same seeds, initial populations, plant behavior, predator behavior, herbivore cooldown (600), and herbivore offspring E (22).

Default: 5 modes × 5 seeds (12345–12349) × 50,000 ticks, snapshots every 100 ticks. Outputs: `results/phase06222/<mode>/seed-<seed>.csv`, per-seed configs, and `results/phase06222/summary.csv`. To rerun one failed seed: `-SeedStart 12348 -SeedCount 1 -Modes control`.

## Analysis plan

1. Compare all five matched seeds per condition, including extinction and 50k survivor counts for herbivores/carnivores, herbivore births and death causes, and N/E ledger errors.
2. Derive *interval* births and gate-block counts by differencing cumulative counters in the windows 0–4000, 4000–8000, 8000–20000, and 20000–50000. Avoid comparing cumulative counters alone because exposure differs with population size.
3. Evaluate phase-lagged plant→herbivore→carnivore peaks, not just final population. No intervention guarantees a stable cycle or prevents natural extinction.
4. The phase20/21 gate counters are diagnostics, some mutually overlapping and others evaluated sequentially. They cannot be interpreted as exclusive causal probabilities. Effects from the config interventions are evidence of sensitivity, not proof of a single mechanistic cause.
