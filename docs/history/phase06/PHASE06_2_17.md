# Phase 06.2.17 — Reproduction cooldown sensitivity and action diagnostics

This phase preserves the Phase 06.2.16 simulation logic except for the configured reproduction cooldown. No predator rescue or forced survival is implemented.

## Conditions (5 paired seeds, 50,000 ticks)

- `control`: reproduction cooldown 600 ticks (06.2.16 baseline).
- `cooldown-300`: reproduction cooldown 300 ticks.
- `cooldown-150`: reproduction cooldown 150 ticks.

**Caution:** `reproduction_cooldown_ticks` also gates initial age eligibility (`age > cooldown`), so these experiments change BOTH age-to-first-birth and inter-birth cooldown. Interpret as a combined effect, not isolated cooldown.

## New cumulative CSV fields

- `phase17_rest_ticks`: predator-ticks with satiety blocking movement/hunting.
- `phase17_active_search_ticks`: predator-ticks with no target, not satiated.
- `phase17_chase_ticks`: predator-ticks with a target.
- `phase17_repro_cooldown_only_ticks`: cooldown blocked but age eligible (other resources may also be insufficient).
- `phase17_repro_age_only_ticks`: age blocked but cooldown zero (other resources may also be insufficient).
- `phase17_repro_resource_ready_ticks`: sufficient reserve nutrients and energy for birth, regardless of other gates.

`phase17_rest_ticks + phase17_active_search_ticks + phase17_chase_ticks` should equal the cumulative predator-ticks processed. These counters are observations only; no RNG calls are added.
