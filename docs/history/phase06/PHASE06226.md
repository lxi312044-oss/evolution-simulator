# Phase 06.2.26 — Satiety gate and post-recovery population dynamics

## Motivation
Phase 06.2.25 restored herbivore body-N from reserve N and greatly increased reproduction, but population overshoot and subsequent collapse remain. Predator survival is not yet restored.

## Scope
- Keep `body_n_repair_per_tick = 0.02` across all four conditions.
- Compare `control` (satiety threshold 0.50, streak 100), `satiety-045` (0.45, 100), `satiety-040` (0.40, 100), `streak-60` (0.50, 60).
- Do not alter plant growth, herbivore feeding, offspring resources, carnivore behavior, or population counts.
- Add non-invasive counters for satiety threshold samples, feeding pause, streak resets and streak completions. Counters are cumulative and should be differenced between CSV rows for per-window rates.
- Note: threshold 0.40 equals feeding-resume fraction; this condition tests the behavioral coupling, not only a numeric gate.

## Decision criteria
Compare herbivore populations and births at 8k/15k/30k/50k ticks, max/peak, plant depletion and rebound, predator extinction time, and N/E conservation. Avoid interpreting larger birth counts alone as success. No artificial population seeding; extinction remains possible.
