# Phase 06.2.27 — Plant–herbivore balance, satiety streak comparison

## Purpose
Phase 06.2.26 suggested 60-tick well-fed streak supports herbivore recovery but can reduce plant populations. Compare **100 / 80 / 60 ticks** under identical seed, body-N repair, plant and predator settings. This phase is a **controlled experiment and analysis update**, not a change to the ecological rules.

## Changes
- `config/phase06227-streak-{100,80,60}.json`: only `herbivores.reproduction_well_fed_ticks` differs (100, 80, 60). Body-N repair remains 0.02 N/tick.
- `run_phase06227.ps1`: 3 conditions × 5 seeds × 50,000 ticks (15 runs), adjustable via parameters.
- `analyze_phase06227.py`: reads existing cumulative diagnostics; exports per-seed outcomes and 5 time-window resource balances. Uses **differences** of cumulative counters, not raw totals.
- No artificial spawning, no predator changes, no modification to conservation or reproduction code.

## Run (PowerShell)
```powershell
cargo test --workspace
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\run_phase06227.ps1" -Ticks 1000 -SeedCount 1
powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\run_phase06227.ps1"
python .\analyze_phase06227.py
```

Outputs: `results/phase06227/{streak-100,streak-80,streak-60}/seed-*.csv`, `per_seed_summary.csv`, `period_resource_balance.csv`.

## Decision criteria
- Herbivore survival at 50k and recovery after 8k.
- Plant birth / age-death / consumed-death trends and available nutrient uptake, especially 15k–50k.
- Peak herbivore population, trough plant population, predator extinction, N/E ledger errors.
- Prefer a setting that supports both herbivore recovery and plant persistence; no assumption that more births equals better ecology.

## Limitations
Plant counts and aggregate N/E flows do not alone establish local edible biomass, and all 5 seeds may be too few to establish robustness. Predator behavior is unchanged; no guarantee of 3-species cycles. Rust compile/run must be validated in the user's environment.
