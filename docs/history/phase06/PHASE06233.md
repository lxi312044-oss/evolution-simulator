# Phase 06.2.33 — Shared behavior policy and inherited defense traits

- Shared `world/behavior.rs` decides Flee, StandGround, Pursue, Explore, Rest from perception and policy.
- Herbivore genes `fear` and `aggression` inherit with small mutations; per-individual `learned_fear` adapts to nearby predator encounters and is **not inherited**.
- Standing ground reduces attack HP damage from 5 to 2.5, but **does not block N/E transfer or inflict damage on predators**. This is defense, not counterattack.
- Carnivores call the same policy for Pursue/Explore/Rest. Their movement mechanics and hunting are still species-specific; full unification is future work.
- The new traits are active when predator evasion is enabled. No global evolution or reinforcement-learning optimizer is claimed.
- `baseline` has evasion off; `evasion` and `defense` currently use identical config and policy, so **they are not distinct experimental conditions**. Do not interpret them as a valid A/B test. A separate defense toggle is required in the next revision.
- Run `cargo test --workspace`; `powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\run_phase06233.ps1 -Ticks 1000 -SeedCount 1`.
- The existing CSV does not yet include fear/aggression gene distributions; add that before drawing evolutionary conclusions.
