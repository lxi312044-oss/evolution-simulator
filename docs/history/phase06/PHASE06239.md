# Phase 06.2.39 — Diagnostic implementation

## User-observed anomalies
1. Herbivores circle predators and jitter between grazing and fleeing. The current behavior has a sharp detection boundary; this phase measures but does not yet fix that switching.
2. Carnivores die despite visible herbivores on the Web. This phase distinguishes prey within twice vision but not within vision, low reserve with prey in vision, and feeding suppression due to satiety. These are hypotheses, not established causes.

## New cumulative CSV columns
- `prey_near_but_outside_vision`: predator tick, prey within 2× vision but none inside vision, predator not satiated.
- `prey_visible_while_low_energy`: prey targeted while reserve energy < 3.
- `prey_near_while_satiated`: prey within 2× vision while satiated.
- `predator_low_energy_no_visible_prey`: reserve energy < 3, no target, not satiated.
- `herbivore_flee_with_plant_visible`: fleeing while a plant target exists.
- `herbivore_threat_near_detection_boundary`: threat detected in outer 15% of detection radius.
- `gene_mutation_trials`: mutation probability succeeded.
