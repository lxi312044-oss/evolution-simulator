# Phase 06.2.40 — Predator death snapshot

## Goal
Determine whether carnivores die from energy exhaustion despite nearby herbivores, without changing dynamics.

## Added cumulative CSV columns
- `p40_death_prey_visible`: nearest herbivore within predator vision at death.
- `p40_death_prey_attack_range`: nearest herbivore within physical attack range at death.
- `p40_death_prey_absent`: no herbivores anywhere in world at death.
- `p40_death_feeding_paused`: feeding_active was false at death.
- `p40_death_nearby_prey_twice_vision`: nearest herbivore within twice vision at death.
- `p40_death_energy_starvation`: death before max age.

Counters are cumulative and overlapping, and capture a single instant at death; they are not causal proof. The old `predator_death_stomach_energy` is a sum over all deaths, not an individual record. This phase does not yet implement individual death event logs, last-hunt timing, or herbivore flee/feed switching counts. Those require state tracking and will be addressed after verifying the death snapshots.
